# v7.4.1 — fixes

- **No more dragging after a tap:** tapping a room on the floor plan to open its heating or light
  controller left the card's room swipe armed, so with a mouse the card followed the pointer to
  another room. Fixed; the controllers' sliders and the plan's dock no longer start a room swipe.
- **A clearer 24 h heating chart:** the target line shows the temperature you set (it used to jump
  to 10 °C whenever Versatile Thermostat went into its safety mode or the central mode paused the
  heating), the heating power is shown as bars per quarter hour, and pauses and the safety mode as
  strips below, with a legend and the temperature range.
